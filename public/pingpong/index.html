Ping Pong Counter

Setup before the game
  1. Place the phone in landscape at the side of the table, level with the table top or a little above it. The whole table should be in view, with the net in the middle.
  2. Press Start camera and keep the phone still. Moving it later means calibrating again.
  3. Tap the video on the table surface, right under the net. The dashed lines move to the net and the table edge. Tap again to adjust.
  4. Check that P1 is on the left, and that the yellow border is on the player serving first. Use Switch server if it isn't.
  5. To serve: hold the ball still for 3 seconds until the status says "to serve", then serve. A ball bounce counts only near the table line.
  6. A serve that touches the net and lands on the other side is a let (orange flash): no point, serve again. If the camera misses it, press Let right away.
